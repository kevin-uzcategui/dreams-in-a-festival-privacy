Dreams in a Festival privacy policy

Applicable version

This policy describes version 0.10.3 of the game for closed testing on Google Play. Publisher: Auradynamics. Contact: https://github.com/kevin-uzcategui/dreams-in-a-festival-privacy/issues. Updated October 7, 2026.

Local progress

Your chosen name, protagonist, progress, characters, inventory and settings are stored on your device. The guest identifier is generated locally. This version does not send that data to an account server and does not provide Google account linking.

Services and permissions

This build does not include advertising, analytics SDKs or advertising tracking. It does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. Internet permission allows external links; playable resources are bundled with the app. Google Play and Android may process information under their own policies.

Purchases

Real-money purchases are disabled. Virtual resources cannot be withdrawn, converted to money or transferred to another user. Summons may produce random outcomes within the game.

Retention and deletion

Progress stays on your device until you delete it, clear app data or uninstall. To remove progress, open Settings, Delete account and confirm deletion. This version has no Auradynamics server account backup or cloud recovery.

Voluntary contact

You can contact support through the public repository. GitHub processes that communication under its own terms. Do not post passwords, payment information or other personal data in a public issue.

Future versions

Before enabling online accounts, synchronization, analytics or payments, we will update this policy and Google Play disclosures to reflect actual data handling.
